# Logo sources

Club crests and the Premier League lion were located through:
https://www.footylogos.com/competition/premier-league

The team-to-image mapping is in src/App.jsx. All 20 current dataset teams use
the corresponding primary crest preview from assets.footylogos.com. The
league mark uses the Premier League SVG listed on the same source page.

These are third-party hosted images, so displaying them requires access to
that host. Team names remain visible independently of image loading.
Logos remain the property of their respective clubs and the Premier League;
this independent forecasting project does not claim ownership or affiliation.
